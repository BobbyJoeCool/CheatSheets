Choosing a Tool — Comparison

There's no single best agent; the right one depends on where you work (terminal, editor, GitHub), how much autonomy you want, which models you can use, and what your organization allows. Many teams use two: an IDE assistant for everyday edits and a terminal or cloud agent for larger tasks. Shared AGENTS.md and MCP servers make switching cheap.

Decision Questions

  • Where do you spend your day: terminal, IDE, or GitHub?
  • Do you need hooks, headless runs, CI?
  • Which models and vendors are approved?
  • Do you need enterprise controls and audit?

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Choosing by benchmark headlines instead of a trial on your own codebase
  • Locking every config into one vendor's format
  • Ignoring data-handling and licensing terms for your org
  • Comparing tools on a single prompt; behavior varies by task type

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Run a two-week pilot with two tools on the same backlog
  • Measure merged PRs and review time, not lines generated
  • Keep instructions and MCP config portable from day one
  • Re-evaluate quarterly; this space changes fast
