⚠ Common Mistakes

  • x & m == 0 in C, Java, JS, C#: parenthesize
  • 1 << 40 with a 32-bit 1: use a 64-bit one
  • Signed bytes: b & 0xFF before shifting
  • Shift ≥ width: undefined or masked

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Use unsigned types for bit patterns
  • Write masks in hex: 0xFF00
  • Prefer built-ins over hand-written loops
  • Name your flags and fields

Quick Summary

  • & — keep · | — set · ^ — flip
  • ~ — invert · << >> — move
  • x & (x - 1) — the most useful trick
