Run the Show From the Keyboard

Play (⌥⌘P) starts the show; the presenter display shows notes, the next slide and a timer on your screen. During a show, type a slide number and press Return to jump, press B or W to blank the screen, and X to swap displays if notes appear on the projector. Rehearse Slideshow practices timing; an iPhone with Keynote works as a remote.

Notes

  • Play › Customize Presenter Display to choose what you see
  • Press ? during a show to list every shortcut
  • Play › Rehearse Slideshow for timed practice
  • Keynote on iPhone / iPad: Keynote Remote controls the show over Wi-Fi
  • Play in Window shows the deck in a window (useful for video calls)

During the Slideshow

⌥⌘P          # play from the current slide
→  Space  N   # next build / slide
←  P          # previous
12 ↩          # go to slide 12
Home / End    # first / last slide
B   W         # black / white screen
X             # swap presenter and audience displays
S             # show / hide slide number
?             # show all shortcuts
H             # hide the show (switch to another app)
Esc  /  Q     # end show

Code Explained

Shortcuts are single keys while a show is running
Swap displays (X) fixes notes showing on the projector
Hidden show (H) lets you switch apps, then click Keynote to resume
Skipped slides can still be reached by number

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Mirroring displays so the audience sees your notes
  • Clicking back through slides during Q&A; type the number
  • Presenting from a file still downloading from iCloud

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Press ? once before your talk to review shortcuts
  • Use B to pause on a black screen for discussion
  • Record a movie version for people who missed it
