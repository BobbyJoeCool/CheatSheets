PERFORM Loops

PERFORM UNTIL · WITH TEST AFTER · PERFORM VARYING … FROM … BY … UNTIL · nested AFTER · inline PERFORM … END-PERFORM · infinite-loop traps

Looping with PERFORM

COBOL loops are all PERFORM: UNTIL repeats while a condition is false, VARYING adds a counter like a for loop, and AFTER nests counters. Inline loops put the body between PERFORM and END-PERFORM instead of in a separate paragraph.

Key Points

  • UNTIL tests before each pass by default (like while)
  • WITH TEST AFTER runs the body at least once (like do-while)
  • The loop ends when the condition becomes true
  • VARYING increments after each pass, then retests

Loop Forms

       01  WS-I        PIC 99.
       01  WS-R        PIC 99.
       01  WS-C        PIC 99.
       01  WS-SUM      PIC 9(5) VALUE 0.

           PERFORM VARYING WS-I FROM 1 BY 1 UNTIL WS-I > 10
              ADD WS-I TO WS-SUM
           END-PERFORM                     *> WS-SUM 55, WS-I 11

           PERFORM 2000-READ-AND-SUM
              UNTIL END-OF-FILE

           PERFORM WITH TEST AFTER
                   UNTIL WS-ANSWER = "Y" OR "N"
              DISPLAY "CONTINUE? (Y/N)"
              ACCEPT WS-ANSWER
           END-PERFORM

           PERFORM VARYING WS-R FROM 1 BY 1 UNTIL WS-R > 3
                     AFTER WS-C FROM 1 BY 1 UNTIL WS-C > 4
              MOVE ZERO TO GRID-CELL(WS-R, WS-C)
           END-PERFORM

           PERFORM VARYING WS-I FROM 10 BY -2 UNTIL WS-I < 1
              DISPLAY WS-I                 *> 10 8 6 4 2
           END-PERFORM

Code Explained

After the loop, WS-I is 11: one past the last value
AFTER is the inner loop: WS-C runs 1–4 for each WS-R
BY -2 counts down
The test-after loop always asks at least once

Modern Equivalent

  • Python for i in range(1, 11):; while not eof:; nested for loops
  • Java for (int i = 1; i <= 10; i++); do { } while (...)
  • JavaScript for (let i = 1; i <= 10; i++); do { } while (...)

Prefer / Avoid

  • counter PIC 9 with UNTIL > 9 → PIC 99 (9 + 1 wraps to 0: infinite loop)
  • out-of-line paragraphs for tiny bodies → inline PERFORM

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Counter too small for the limit + 1, so it wraps and loops forever
  • Forgetting to read the next record inside an UNTIL END-OF-FILE loop
  • Changing the VARYING counter inside the body

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Size counters one digit bigger than the limit needs
  • Use COMP counters for speed in tight loops
  • Use EXIT PERFORM instead of GO TO to leave a loop early

Quick Summary

  • UNTIL — while not
  • WITH TEST AFTER — do-while
  • VARYING … AFTER — nested for
  • inline — PERFORM … END-PERFORM
