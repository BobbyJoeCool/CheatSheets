MULTIPLY & DIVIDE

MULTIPLY … BY · DIVIDE … INTO / BY · GIVING · REMAINDER · ROUNDED · divide by zero & SIZE ERROR

Multiplication and Division

MULTIPLY A BY B stores the product in B; DIVIDE A INTO B divides B by A and stores it in B. The GIVING forms put the result somewhere else, and REMAINDER captures what is left over. Division by zero is a size error.

Key Points

  • INTO divides the second operand by the first
  • BY (with GIVING) reads left to right: A / B
  • REMAINDER needs GIVING
  • Divide by zero without SIZE ERROR abends (S0CB)

MULTIPLY and DIVIDE Forms

       01  WS-QTY      PIC 9(3)      VALUE 12.
       01  WS-PRICE    PIC 9(3)V99   VALUE 2.50.
       01  WS-COST     PIC 9(5)V99.
       01  WS-TOTAL    PIC 9(5)V99   VALUE 100.
       01  WS-AVG      PIC 9(3)V99.
       01  WS-BOXES    PIC 9(3).
       01  WS-LEFT     PIC 9(3).
       01  WS-ZERO     PIC 9         VALUE 0.

           MULTIPLY WS-QTY BY WS-PRICE GIVING WS-COST    *> 30.00
           MULTIPLY 2 BY WS-QTY                          *> 24
           DIVIDE 3 INTO WS-TOTAL GIVING WS-AVG ROUNDED  *> 33.33
           DIVIDE WS-TOTAL BY 3 GIVING WS-AVG            *> 33.33
           DIVIDE WS-QTY BY 5 GIVING WS-BOXES
                  REMAINDER WS-LEFT                      *> 4 r 4
           DIVIDE WS-TOTAL BY WS-ZERO GIVING WS-AVG
              ON SIZE ERROR
                 DISPLAY "DIVIDE BY ZERO"
           END-DIVIDE

Code Explained

MULTIPLY 2 BY WS-QTY changes WS-QTY in place
DIVIDE 3 INTO WS-TOTAL = WS-TOTAL / 3
24 / 5 = 4 boxes with 4 left over
The size error branch catches the zero divisor

Modern Equivalent

  • Python cost = qty * price; divmod(24, 5) → (4, 4)
  • Java price.multiply(qty); 24 / 5 and 24 % 5; ArithmeticException on /0
  • JavaScript qty * price; Math.trunc(24 / 5) and 24 % 5; /0 gives Infinity

Prefer / Avoid

  • mixing INTO and BY → COMPUTE for anything non-trivial
  • unchecked division → ON SIZE ERROR or test the divisor first

Forms

StatementResult
MULTIPLY a BY bb = a × b
MULTIPLY a BY b GIVING cc = a × b
DIVIDE a INTO bb = b / a
DIVIDE a INTO b GIVING cc = b / a
DIVIDE a BY b GIVING cc = a / b
… REMAINDER rr = leftover

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Swapping INTO and BY and dividing the wrong way
  • Writing MULTIPLY A BY 2 (a literal cannot receive the result)
  • Dividing by a field that can be zero with no check (S0CB abend)

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Read DIVIDE A INTO B as "A goes into B"
  • Use ROUNDED on averages and rates
  • Use REMAINDER for packing, paging and batching counts

Quick Summary

  • MULTIPLY … BY — product
  • DIVIDE … INTO — b / a
  • DIVIDE … BY … GIVING — a / b
  • REMAINDER — leftover
