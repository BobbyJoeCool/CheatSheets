Sheet 17

MOVE

MOVE … TO · padding & truncation · decimal-point alignment · group moves · MOVE CORRESPONDING · multiple receivers

Copying Data Between Fields

MOVE copies a value into one or more fields, converting it to fit each receiver. Alphanumeric receivers are filled left to right and padded with spaces; numeric receivers line up on the decimal point and pad with zeros. Anything that does not fit is cut off silently.

Key Points

  • The source is unchanged; the receiver is overwritten
  • Text: truncated on the right
  • Numbers: integer digits cut on the left, decimals on the right
  • Group moves are raw byte copies with no conversion

Under the Hood

  • A group MOVE compiles to a single MVC instruction (byte copy)
  • Numeric to numeric moves may PACK/UNPK to convert USAGE
  • Moving alphanumeric to numeric copies bytes: non-digits cause later S0C7

MOVE Rules in Action

       01  WS-NAME     PIC X(5).
       01  WS-NUM      PIC 9(3)V99.
       01  WS-SMALL    PIC 99.
       01  WS-OUT      PIC 9(5).
       01  A-REC.
           05 CUST-ID  PIC 9(4).
           05 AMOUNT   PIC 9(5)V99.
       01  B-REC.
           05 AMOUNT   PIC 9(5)V99.
           05 CUST-ID  PIC 9(4).

           MOVE "AB" TO WS-NAME         *> "AB   "
           MOVE "ABCDEFG" TO WS-NAME    *> "ABCDE"
           MOVE 12.5 TO WS-NUM          *> 012.50
           MOVE 1234.567 TO WS-NUM      *> 234.56
           MOVE 1234 TO WS-SMALL        *> 34
           MOVE WS-NUM TO WS-OUT        *> 00234 (decimals dropped)
           MOVE ZERO TO WS-NUM WS-OUT   *> two receivers
           MOVE CORRESPONDING A-REC TO B-REC
           MOVE CUST-ID OF A-REC TO WS-OUT  *> qualified name

Code Explained

Short text is padded with spaces on the right
Numbers align on the decimal point; extra digits fall off both ends
MOVE CORRESPONDING moves fields with matching names, in any order
OF (or IN) qualifies a name used in more than one record

Modern Equivalent

  • Python b = a; slicing s[:5].ljust(5) mimics PIC X(5)
  • Java b = a; fixed-width needs String.format("%-5.5s", s)
  • JavaScript b = a; s.slice(0, 5).padEnd(5) mimics PIC X(5)

Prefer / Avoid

  • silent truncation → size receivers to the largest value
  • MOVE CORRESPONDING on big records → explicit moves (clearer)

Move Rules

From → ToResult
X → XLeft-justify, pad spaces, cut right
9 → 9Align point, pad zeros, cut both ends
9 → XDigits as text (unsigned integer)
X → 9Bytes copied; must be digits
9 → editedFormatted for output
group → anyByte copy, no conversion

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Moving a 7-digit total into a PIC 9(5) field and losing the high digits
  • Moving text into a numeric field and doing math on it later (S0C7)
  • Expecting a group move to convert COMP-3 to readable digits

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Compile with warnings to catch truncating moves
  • Use READ … INTO and WRITE … FROM instead of extra moves
  • Qualify duplicate names with OF to avoid ambiguity errors

Quick Summary

  • MOVE a TO b c — copy to receivers
  • text — pad/cut right
  • numbers — align on the point
  • group — raw bytes
