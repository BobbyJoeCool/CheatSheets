Quotes, Callouts & Rules

A blockquote prefixes lines with > and can hold any block: paragraphs, lists, headings, code. Nest with >>. GitHub alerts turn a blockquote into a colored callout when its first line is [!NOTE], [!TIP], [!IMPORTANT], [!WARNING] or [!CAUTION]. A horizontal rule is 3 or more -, * or _ on a line by itself.

Rules

  • Lazy continuation: a line without > still continues the quoted paragraph
  • A blank line ends the quote; a lone > keeps it going
  • Keep the alert marker alone on its line; content goes on the next lines

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Two quotes with only a blank line between them — some renderers merge them; add text or <!-- -->
  • --- right under a paragraph — becomes an h2, not a rule
  • Alerts outside GitHub — other renderers show the literal [!NOTE]
  • Text on the [!NOTE] line — GitHub then renders a plain quote

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Use alerts sparingly — GitHub's docs suggest one or two per page
  • Use a lone > to keep multi-paragraph quotes together
  • Use --- for rules consistently (markdownlint MD035)
  • Prefer headings over rules to structure a document

Quick Summary

  • > [!type]- — Obsidian callout, foldable
