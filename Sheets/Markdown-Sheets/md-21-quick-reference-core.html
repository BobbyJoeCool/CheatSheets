⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Missing blank lines between blocks — lists, headings and tables merge into paragraphs
  • No space after #, - or 1. — not parsed as syntax
  • Wrong indentation in nested lists — items flatten or turn into code

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • The # column points to the sheet with full details
  • Badges: GFM Pandoc Obsidian Ext mark non-CommonMark syntax
  • When in doubt, preview in the tool you're publishing to
