⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Copying VS Code habits: ⌘D here duplicates, ⌥↑ extends selection
  • On Windows, Ctrl+Y deletes a line — it's not redo
  • Assuming a custom keymap matches this sheet — check Settings › Keymap

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Print Help › Keymap Reference for your exact keymap
  • Use Find Action when a shortcut slips your mind
