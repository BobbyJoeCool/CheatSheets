Working Days, Markers & Timelines

Gantt charts can skip non-working days with excludes, control axis ticks with tickInterval, style or hide the today line, and add vertical markers (vert) for deadlines. A timeline is the simpler cousin: no dates or durations, just periods (any text) with one or more events, optionally grouped into sections. Use Gantt for plans you schedule, timeline for history you tell.

Notes

  • excludes takes dates, day names or weekends — not weekdays
  • Excluded days extend a task's bar instead of being counted
  • weekend friday makes Friday–Saturday the weekend (v11)
  • Timeline text is free-form: 2024 Q1, Stone Age, v1.0
  • timeline TD draws top-down (v11.14)

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Writing excludes weekdays to show weekends only. Only weekends, day names and dates are allowed.
  • Using a timeline when tasks have real start and end dates — timelines don't scale by time; use Gantt.
  • Indenting timeline events under a period on a new line without the leading :. Continuation lines must start with :.

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Add excludes weekends to any work plan so durations mean working days.
  • Use vert for fixed dates (code freeze, conference) that aren't tasks.
  • Keep timeline periods short (2024, Q3) and put detail in the events.

Quick Summary

  • excludes / weekend — working calendar
  • tickInterval / axisFormat — axis control
  • vert / todayMarker — reference lines
  • timeline + period : event — simple history
