Treemap beta

treemap-beta · indentation · "Section" · "Leaf": value · :::class · valueFormat

Hierarchy Sized by Value

A treemap-beta shows a hierarchy as nested rectangles whose area is proportional to value. Like a mindmap, indentation creates the hierarchy. Section (parent) nodes are just a quoted name; leaf nodes are a quoted name, a colon and a number. Parents are sized by the total of their leaves. Style nodes with :::class and classDef, and format numbers with valueFormat.

Notes

  • All names in double quotes
  • Only leaves get values; sections sum their children
  • Indent with spaces or tabs — but consistently
  • Syntax may still change (beta)

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Giving a section its own value. Only leaves carry numbers; a section's size is the sum of its children.
  • Forgetting quotes around names. Unquoted names fail to parse.
  • Using a treemap for flat data with no hierarchy — a bar chart is easier to read.

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Use treemaps for "where does it go" questions: disk usage, cloud costs, bundle size.
  • Limit depth to two or three levels so labels still fit inside rectangles.
  • Highlight the one or two items under discussion with a class; leave the rest themed.
