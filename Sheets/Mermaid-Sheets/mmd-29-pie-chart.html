Pie Chart

pie · showData · title · "label" : value · donutHole · textPosition · pie1–pie12

Share of a Whole

A pie chart shows how a total splits into parts. After the declaration (optionally showData to print values in the legend) and a title, each line is a quoted label, a colon and a positive number. Slices are drawn clockwise in the order you write them. Since v11.16, config can turn it into a donut, move the legend and highlight a slice.

Notes

  • Values must be positive; up to two decimals
  • Labels must be in double quotes
  • Mermaid works out the percentages
  • title can sit on the declaration line: pie title Pets

Browser Share

---
config:
  pie:
    donutHole: 0.4
    textPosition: 0.8
---
pie showData

Key Effect
textPosition: 0.75 Label position, 0 center → 1 edge
donutHole: 0.4 Donut, 0 – 0.9 (v11.16)
legendPosition: bottom top bottom left right center
highlightSlice: Safari Emphasize one slice (or hover)

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Leaving labels unquoted (Chrome : 61). Pie labels must be in double quotes.
  • Using negative numbers or zero for a slice; pie values must be positive.
  • Using a pie for more than about six slices or for data that isn't parts of one whole. Use xychart bars (sheet 31).

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Order slices largest first so the chart reads clockwise from the biggest share.
  • Group small values into an "Other" slice.
  • Add showData when exact numbers matter; the legend then shows each value.
