Crow's Foot Notation

A relationship line reads A <left-marker><line><right-marker> B : label. Each marker has two characters: the outer one is the maximum (| one, {/} many) and the inner one is the minimum (o zero, | one). The line is -- for an identifying relationship (solid) or .. for non-identifying (dashed). The label is required and describes the relationship from left to right.

Reading a Line

  • CUSTOMER ||--o{ ORDER — one customer, zero or more orders
  • Outer char = maximum, inner char = minimum
  • Left marker describes the left entity's count
  • Labels with spaces need quotes: : "is billed to"
  • Word aliases exist: 1 to zero or more

Lines & Patterns

Syntax Meaning
-- Identifying (solid line)
.. Non-identifying (dashed)
A ||--o{ B One-to-many
A ||--|| B One-to-one
A }o--o{ B Many-to-many
A |o--|| B Optional one-to-one
A ||--o{ A Self-reference
A ||--o{ B : "has many" Quoted label

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Reading the marker backwards. In A ||--o{ B the o{ sits next to B, so it says how many B each A has.
  • Leaving out the label. A ||--o{ B alone is a syntax error; add : has (or : "").
  • Using .. just to make a line look lighter. Dashed means non-identifying: the child can exist without the parent.

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Resolve many-to-many with a join entity (ORDER_LINE) — it matches how the tables are really built.
  • Write labels as verbs that read left to right: CUSTOMER places ORDER.
  • Keep the parent entity on the left so every line reads the same way.

Quick Summary

  • A <m><line><m> B : label — relationship statement
  • |o || }o }| — zero/one, one, zero+, one+
  • -- / .. — identifying / non-identifying
  • : label — required, reads left to right
