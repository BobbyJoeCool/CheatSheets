Flows Between Stages

A sankey diagram shows quantities flowing from sources to targets; link width is proportional to the value. The body is plain CSV with exactly three columns: source,target,value. A node that appears as both a target and a source becomes a middle stage. Empty lines are allowed for readability, and names with commas or quotes follow CSV quoting rules.

Notes

  • Exactly 3 columns per line: source, target, value
  • Commas inside a name: wrap it in "…"
  • Quote inside a quoted name: double it ""
  • sankey-beta still works as an alias

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Adding a header row (source,target,value). It's read as data; comment it out with %%.
  • Using spaces after commas (A, B, 10). They become part of the names, creating separate nodes.
  • Making a loop (A → B → A). Sankey flows must not form cycles; the diagram fails to render.

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Export the CSV straight from a spreadsheet and paste it under sankey.
  • Use linkColor: gradient when you want to trace flows across several stages.
  • Round values; the picture is about proportions, not decimals.

Quick Summary
