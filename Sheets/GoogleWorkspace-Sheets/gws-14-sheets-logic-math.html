Decide and Summarise

IF, IFS and SWITCH pick values; the …IFS family (SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIFS, MAXIFS, MINIFS) adds up or counts rows that meet conditions. They behave exactly like Excel's, so formulas move between the two without changes. SUMPRODUCT handles array math and complex conditions.

Notes

  • Criteria are text: ">100", "<>Closed", ">="&F1
  • Wildcards in criteria: "*north*"; escape with ~
  • SUMIFS: sum range first, then range / criteria pairs
  • Sheets also has COUNTUNIQUE and COUNTUNIQUEIFS
  • IFNA is safer than IFERROR around lookups

Logic & Conditional Math

=IF(C2>=70, "Pass", "Fail")
=IFS(C2>=90,"A", C2>=80,"B", C2>=70,"C", TRUE,"F")
=SWITCH(B2, "N","North", "S","South", "Other")
=IF(AND(B2="West", C2>1000), "Big West", "")
=SUMIFS(Sales!D:D, Sales!B:B,"West", Sales!A:A,">="&F1)
=COUNTIFS(A:A,"Open", B:B,"<"&TODAY())   # overdue open items
=AVERAGEIFS(C:C, B:B, "Widget")
=MAXIFS(C:C, B:B, "Widget")              # also MINIFS
=COUNTUNIQUEIFS(Customer, Region, "West")  # distinct customers
=SUMPRODUCT(Qty, Price)                  # Σ qty × price
=ROUND(B2*1.0825, 2)
=IFERROR(B2/C2, 0)

Code Explained

TRUE, value at the end of IFS acts as "else"
Join cell values into criteria with &
COUNTUNIQUEIFS counts distinct values that meet conditions (no Excel equivalent)
Whole-column refs like D:D are fine in Sheets for moderate data sizes

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Putting the sum range last (old SUMIF order) in SUMIFS
  • Writing ">F1" instead of ">"&F1
  • IFERROR around everything, hiding broken references

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Drive reports from input cells (">="&StartDate)
  • Use COUNTUNIQUE for "how many customers" instead of pivots
  • Prefer IFS or SWITCH to nested IFs

Quick Summary

  • IF / IFS / SWITCH — choose
  • SUMIFS — sum range first
  • COUNTUNIQUEIFS — distinct counts
  • IFNA — lookup misses only
