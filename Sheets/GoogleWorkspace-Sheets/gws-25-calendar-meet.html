Calendar & Meet

Calendar shortcuts c t d w m · find a time · appointment schedules · working hours & location · time insights · Meet shortcuts

Schedule and Meet Faster

Google Calendar has single-key shortcuts (c create, t today, d / w / m views). Find a time shows attendees' availability side by side; appointment schedules give people a booking page. Working hours & location tells others when and where you work. In Meet, shortcuts handle mute, camera and hand raise.

Notes

  • Shortcuts are on by default in Calendar; ? lists them
  • Appointment schedules: Create › Appointment schedule (booking page; more options on paid plans)
  • Settings › Working hours & location; add focus time and out of office
  • Time insights (left panel) shows how your time is split
  • Meet: Activities › Recording / Transcripts / Take notes for me (plan dependent)

Automate It

  • Appointment schedule link in your email signature instead of back-and-forth
  • Focus time events that auto-decline meetings

Calendar & Meet Keys

# Calendar
c             # create event          e      # event details
t             # today                 /      # search
d w m y       # day, week, month, year   x   # custom view
j / k  or n / p   # next / previous period
q             # quick add             ⌫ / Delete    # delete event

# Meet
⌘D            / Ctrl+D          # mute / unmute
⌘E            / Ctrl+E          # camera on / off
⌃⌘H           / Ctrl+Alt+H      # raise / lower hand
CC button     / CC button       # captions

Code Explained

Quick add (q or typing in the event box) understands "Lunch with Sam Friday 12pm"
x cycles your custom view (e.g. 4 days)
Meet shortcuts work while the Meet tab has focus
Out of office events auto-decline meetings during that time

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Booking across time zones without checking the attendee's zone
  • Leaving appointment schedules open with no buffer between meetings
  • Forgetting you're unmuted; learn ⌘D / Ctrl+D

✓ Tips & Best Practices

  • Add a secondary time zone in Settings
  • Set default event length to 25 / 50 minutes (Speedy meetings)
  • Attach the agenda doc to the invite
